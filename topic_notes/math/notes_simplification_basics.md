Simplification &amp; Approximation – Sir&apos;s Basic Method (Notes)
=============================================================

Source idea: old 2025 PDFs – &quot;Simplification &amp; Approximation Part – 1&quot; and related files.  
Level: Pre / Foundation – equations with one &quot;?&quot; and BODMAS.

Main Types of Questions
-----------------------

1) Simple linear expressions:
   - 12 + 15 + 17 = ? + 15  
   - 12 + 15 − ? = 20  
   - ? + 36 = 72 − 20  

2) Simple +/− with unknown:
   - 50 − ? = 12  
   - 100 − ? + 25 = 75  
   - 24 − ? + 18 = 30  

3) × and ÷ with unknown:
   - 30 × ? − 25 = 55  
   - ? + 50 − 25 = 75  
   - ? × 5 − 15 = 25  
   - 60 − ? × 3 = 30  

4) Mix of ×, ÷, +, − and brackets:
   - 7200 ÷ ? ÷ 9 ÷ 8 = 10  
   - 15 + 36 ÷ 6 = ?  
   - 36 − ? ÷ 6 = 30  
   - 40 − (10 − 20) = ?  
   - 10 − (10 − ?) = 80  

5) Fractional forms:
   - (25 + 15) ÷ (5 + ?) = 12  
   - 10 + 20 ÷ (5 + ?) = 14  
   - (36 ÷ 6 + ?) × 5 = 40  

Sir&apos;s Standard Approach
--------------------------

**1. Always respect BODMAS**

Order:
- Brackets  
- Of (%, fractions)  
- Division  
- Multiplication  
- Addition  
- Subtraction  

Example:  
15 + 36 ÷ 6 = ?  

Step:
- First 36 ÷ 6 = 6  
- Then 15 + 6 = 21  
Answer: ? = 21

**2. Treat &quot;?&quot; like x and isolate it (inverse operations)**

Example:  
12 + 15 + 17 = ? + 15  

Total LHS: 12 + 15 + 17 = 44  
Equation: 44 = ? + 15  

To get ? alone:
- ? = 44 − 15 = 29  

So ? = 29

Example:  
12 + 15 − ? = 20  

LHS: 27 − ? = 20  
Move 20 to LHS: 27 − 20 = ?  
? = 7  

Shortcut thinking:
- Start from 20, add those which &quot;went away&quot;:
- 20 + 15 = 35  
- 35 − 12 = 23 (not good here, so better do equation way)  

Right way as sir would say:  
27 − ? = 20 → subtract 20 from 27.

**3. If ? is being multiplied, divide to isolate**

Example:  
30 × ? − 25 = 55  

Step‑1: Move −25 to RHS → 30×? = 55 + 25 = 80  
Step‑2: ? = 80 ÷ 30 = 8/3  

In many pre‑level questions options will be 8/3, 2, 3, etc.

Example:  
? × 5 − 15 = 25  

Step‑1: ?×5 = 25 + 15 = 40  
Step‑2: ? = 40 ÷ 5 = 8  

**4. If ? is in division, multiply to isolate**

Example:  
? ÷ 25 + 40 = 44  

Step‑1: ? ÷ 25 = 44 − 40 = 4  
Step‑2: ? = 4 × 25 = 100  

Example:  
36 − ? ÷ 6 = 30  

Step‑1: −? ÷ 6 = 30 − 36 = −6  
Step‑2: ? ÷ 6 = 6  
Step‑3: ? = 6 × 6 = 36  

**5. Chain division like 7200 ÷ ? ÷ 9 ÷ 8**

Example from PDF:  
7200 ÷ ? ÷ 9 ÷ 8 = 10  

Interpretation: (((7200 ÷ ?) ÷ 9) ÷ 8) = 10  

Work from RHS backwards:

Let X = 7200 ÷ ?  

Then: X ÷ 9 ÷ 8 = 10  

First reverse divide by 8:  
X ÷ 9 = 10 × 8 = 80  

Then reverse divide by 9:  
X = 80 × 9 = 720  

Now X = 7200 ÷ ? = 720  

So ? = 7200 ÷ 720 = 10  

Sir का तरीका:  
- 10 → ×8 → 80 → ×9 → 720  
- 7200 ÷ ? = 720 → ? = 7200 / 720 = 10  

**6. Brackets: Solve inside first, then outside**

Example:  
40 − (10 − 20) = ?  

Inside bracket: 10 − 20 = −10  
So expression = 40 − (−10) = 40 + 10 = 50  

Example:  
10 − (10 − ?) = 80  

Step‑1: Let inner (10 − ?) = A  
Then 10 − A = 80 → A = 10 − 80 = −70  

Now 10 − ? = −70 → −? = −70 − 10 = −80 → ? = 80  

So answer = 80

Sir usually कहेंगे:
- &quot;Minus bracket&quot; को sign change करके खोलो।


Typical Shortcuts to Remember
-----------------------------

1. If equation looks like:  
   - a + b + c = ? + b  
   → simply add a, b, c then subtract b once.

2. If pattern is like:  
   - total − ? = given  
   → ? = total − given  

3. For &quot;? × k = number&quot;  
   → ? = number ÷ k  

4. For &quot;? ÷ k = number&quot;  
   → ? = number × k  

5. For &quot;(A − ?) ÷ k = B&quot;  
   → A − ? = Bk → ? = A − Bk  

6. For &quot;A ÷ (B + ?) = C&quot;  
   → B + ? = A ÷ C → ? = (A ÷ C) − B  


Practice Template (How to use these notes)
------------------------------------------

When you see such questions in LIVE/Practice:

1. First step in mind:  
   - &quot;Is this only +/− ? ⇒ I will combine constants first.&quot;  
   - &quot;Is there × or ÷ with ? ⇒ I will shift other terms and then divide/multiply.&quot;  
   - &quot;Are there brackets ⇒ I will solve bracket first using BODMAS.&quot;

2. Try to solve without writing full equation, but logically:
   - Move numbers across &quot;=&quot; by opposite operation  
   - Keep sign carefully (+/−)

3. After solving, quickly plug back to check:
   - LHS == RHS?
