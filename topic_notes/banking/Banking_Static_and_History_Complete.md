## 1. Basic Idea of a Bank

**Bank kya karta hai?**

- Public se **paisa leta hai** → **Deposits** (saving, current, FD, RD)  
- Usi paisa ko **aage loan deta hai** → **Advances** (home loan, personal loan, etc.)  
- Beech ka difference (interest rate) + fees → **Bank ka profit**  

Isliye bank ko bola जाता है:

- **Financial Intermediary** – बीच वाला जो बचत करने वालों aur borrow करने वालों ko connect करे.

## 2. Types of Banks in India

### 2.1 Scheduled vs Non‑Scheduled Bank

**Scheduled Bank**

- वो bank जिसका नाम **RBI Act, 1934 ke Second Schedule** me hai.  
- Condition:  
  - Paid‑up capital + reserves ≥ ₹5 lakh (old condition, exam में line याद: “minimum paid‑up capital and reserves of such an amount as RBI may specify”).  
- Benefits:
  - RBI se refinance / borrow कर सकता है  
  - Clearing house facility  
  - RBI ka supervision strong

**Non‑Scheduled Bank**

- Jo Second Schedule me नहीं hai.  
- छोटे cooperative ya local area banks etc.  
- Exam में ज़्यादा detail नहीं, बस difference याद रखना.

### 2.2 Commercial Banks ke Types

1. **Public Sector Banks (PSB)**  
   - Govt share ≥ 51%  
   - e.g. State Bank of India, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, etc.

2. **Private Sector Banks**  
   - Majority share private promoters ke paas  
   - e.g. HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank

3. **Foreign Banks**  
   - Head office विदेश में, branches India में  
   - e.g. Citi, HSBC, Standard Chartered

4. **Regional Rural Banks (RRB)**  
   - Rural area ke liye, agriculture + small borrowers ko loan  
   - Sponsor bank (PSB) + State Govt + Central Govt milke set up karte hain  
   - Shareholding pattern:  
     - Central Govt – 50%  
     - State Govt – 15%  
     - Sponsor Bank – 35%

5. **Co‑operative Banks**  
   - “Co‑operative Society” ke form me, “one person one vote” principle  
   - Mainly rural/urban co‑op credit societies ke through loan

## 3. Main Banking Products (Deposits & Loans)

### 3.1 Deposit Accounts

1. **Savings Account**

   - Normal public ke liye  
   - Interest मिलता है (low to moderate)  
   - Withdrawal allowed (ATM, cheque, UPI आदि)

2. **Current Account**

   - Business / companies ke liye  
   - Generally **no interest**  
   - Unlimited transactions, overdraft facility possible

3. **Fixed Deposit (FD)**

   - Fixed time ke liye deposit (7 days se 10–20 years)  
   - Higher interest rate  
   - Premature withdrawal pe **penalty** लग सकती है

4. **Recurring Deposit (RD)**

   - Har month fixed amount deposit  
   - End me lump sum + interest  
   - Use: salaried log jo धीरे‑धीरे बचत करना चाहते हैं

**Simple link:**

- Savings / Current → **Demand deposits** (kabhi bhi माँग सकते ho)  
- FD / RD → **Term/Time deposits** (fixed period).

### 3.2 Loan / Advances

1. **Term Loan**

   - Fixed period ke लिये loan (1–3 saal short term; 3–7 saal medium; 7+ long term)  
   - EMI me repayment – home loan, car loan, etc.

2. **Cash Credit (CC)**

   - Mainly business ke liye  
   - Firm ko limit दी जाती है (e.g. ₹50 lakh)  
   - Stock / inventory ke against  
   - Jitna use karega, utne par interest

3. **Overdraft (OD)**

   - Aap account me जितना balance hai usse ज़्यादा निकाल सकते ho up to limit  
   - Salary OD, current OD, etc.

4. **Bill Discounting**

   - Business ne goods बेचे aur bill banaya (invoice).  
   - Bank उस bill ka paisa abhi दे deta hai (thoda interest कटकर)  
   - Later buyer se full amount लेता hai.

## 4. Important Banking Terms (One‑line Definitions)

### 4.1 CRR and SLR

1. **CRR – Cash Reserve Ratio**

   - Bank apne **NDTL (Net Demand and Time Liabilities)** ka kuch %  
     **RBI ke paas cash** me rakhte hain.  
   - Is par bank ko interest नहीं मिलता.  
   - Tool of **monetary policy** – liquidity control.

2. **SLR – Statutory Liquidity Ratio**

   - NDTL ka % jo bank ko **self ke paas liquid assets** me rakhna होता hai:  
     - cash, gold, approved securities (mainly Govt securities).  
   - RBI fix करता है (under Banking Regulation Act).

**Connection:**

- CRR → पैसा RBI ke पास  
- SLR → पैसा bank ke पास but locked assets me  
- Dono मिलकर decide करते hain कि bank kitna paisa loan दे सकता hai.

### 4.2 Repo, Reverse Repo, Bank Rate, MSF

1. **Repo Rate**

   - Short term me **RBI se loan** lene par jo interest rate bank pay करते hain  
   - Against Govt securities – **repurchase agreement**.  
   - RBI agar repo बढ़ाए → loan महँगा → liquidity कम.

2. **Reverse Repo Rate**

   - Bank agar अपना extra paisa RBI ke पास park करे → RBI jo interest दे → Reverse Repo.  
   - RBI liquidity **absorb** करने ke लिये use करता hai.

3. **Bank Rate**

   - Long term borrowing ke लिये RBI ka rate (earlier without repo agreement).  
   - Signalling rate for long‑term loans, penalty, etc.

4. **MSF – Marginal Standing Facility**

   - Emergency window – bank **overnight RBI se borrow** कर सकते हैं  
   - Repo se थोड़ी high rate  
   - Jab SLR securities me se thoda extra use karke borrowing करनी हो.

**Simple memory:**

- Repo → “RBI se पैसा LOAN”  
- Reverse Repo → “RBI ko पैसा DEPOSIT”  
- MSF → “Last minute रात वाला loan”

## 5. Cheque, Draft, RTGS, NEFT, IMPS, UPI

### 5.1 Cheque

- Written आदेश by account holder to bank:  
  - “Pay ₹X to Mr. Y”  
- Types:
  - **Bearer cheque** – jisके हाथ me cheque, usko payment.  
  - **Order cheque** – named person ko payment.  
  - **Crossed cheque** – sirf account me credit, cash नहीं.

### 5.2 Demand Draft (DD)

- Bank स्वयं issue करता hai, **pre‑paid instrument**  
- Customer bank ko paisa देता hai → bank DD बनाकर kisi third person ke नाम je karta hai.  
- Cheque se ज़्यादा safe (क्योंकि bank की liability)

### 5.3 Electronic Payment Systems

1. **RTGS – Real Time Gross Settlement**

   - **High‑value** real‑time transfer  
   - No netting (each transaction separate)  
   - Minimum amount (earlier ₹2 lakh) – exam me concept important.

2. **NEFT – National Electronic Funds Transfer**

   - Batch based system (half‑hourly batches – ab almost near real time).  
   - कोई minimum limit नहीं, suitable for normal transfers.

3. **IMPS – Immediate Payment Service**

   - 24×7, instant, generally up to a certain limit (banks decide).  
   - Used heavily in net banking, mobile banking.

4. **UPI – Unified Payments Interface**

   - Mobile‐based platform – simple ID (VPA) se payment.  
   - Developed by **NPCI**.  
   - Supports P2P (person to person) और P2M (merchant) both.

## 6. Priority Sector Lending (PSL)

**Idea:** Certain sectors को सरकार चाहती है कि banks ज़रूर loan दें – samajik aur vikas ke लिये.

Major PSL Categories:

- Agriculture  
- Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)  
- Education  
- Housing (small value)  
- Export credit  
- Others: Renewable energy, social infrastructure, etc.

**Target:**  
- Domestic commercial banks: PSL = **40% of ANBC** (Adjusted Net Bank Credit) approx.  
- RRB / SFB ke liye अलग norms.

## 7. RBI – Role & Structure (Short)

**RBI – Reserve Bank of India**

- **Central Bank** of India (established 1935, nationalised 1949).  

Main Functions:

1. **Issuer of Currency** (except ₹1 note & coins – Govt issues).  
2. **Banker to Government** (central + state).  
3. **Banker’s Bank** – commercial banks ko lending/refinance, clearing house.  
4. **Monetary Authority** – inflation + growth balance, policy rates (Repo, CRR, etc.).  
5. **Regulator of Banking System** – licences, inspections, guidelines.  
6. **Foreign Exchange Management** – under FEMA, manages forex reserves.

**Connection with other bodies:**

- **NABARD** – agriculture & rural dev bank (RBI + Govt created).  
- **SIDBI** – MSME financing.  
- **NHB** (ab merged with RBI) – housing finance.  
- **EXIM Bank** – export‑import finance.

## 8. NPA, Provisioning, Basel Norms (Basic)

### 8.1 NPA – Non‑Performing Asset

- Jab borrower **interest/principal 90 days se ज़्यादा overdue** ho jata hai → asset becomes NPA.  

Categories (exam mein नाम):

- **Sub-standard** – NPA up to 12 months  
- **Doubtful** – NPA for 12+ months  
- **Loss** – uncollectible, but not written off fully

**Provisioning:**  
- Bank ko इन loans ke against kuch paisa side me rakhna पड़ता hai (expense) → capital safety.

### 8.2 Basel Norms (very short)

- International norms for **capital adequacy**, risk management.  
- Current: **Basel III** implement ho raha hai.  
- CRAR/CAR (Capital to Risk‑weighted Assets Ratio) → India me minimum ~10.875% (inc. CCB).

## 9. Small Important Definitions (Memory Box)

- **KYC – Know Your Customer**  
  - Customer identification process (ID, address, photo) to prevent money laundering.

- **AML – Anti Money Laundering**  
  - Laws + policies to stop illegal money being routed through banking.

- **Financial Inclusion**  
  - गरीब / unbanked log tak banking services पहुंचाना (Jan Dhan, PMJDY, BC model, etc.)

- **Business Correspondent (BC)**  
  - Bank ka agent जो दूर‑दराज क्षेत्रों में छोटा banking काम करता है (opening account, cash in/out, etc.).

- **Payment Bank**  
  - Bank जो deposit ले सकता hai (up to limit, earlier ₹1 lakh)  
  - लेकिन loan नहीं दे सकता; केवल payment/ remittance सेवाएँ.

- **Small Finance Bank (SFB)**  
  - छोटी income groups, small business ko loan देने ke लिए shape kiya गया bank,  
  - Full banking licence but focus on small borrowers.
