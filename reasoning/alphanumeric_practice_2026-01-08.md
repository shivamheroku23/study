## Answers

**Set 1**

Q1. I  
- Letters: +2 pattern → A,C,E,G,I,K  

Q2. 15  
- +3 pattern  

Q3. J5, L6  
- Letters: +2; numbers: +1  

Q4. 10I, 12K  
- Numbers: +2; letters: +2 positions  

Q5. B  
- P(16), S(19), V(22), Y(25) → +3; next 28 → 28−26=2 → B  

---

**Set 2**

Q6. (a) E9  
- Numbers: +2; letters: +1  

Q7. (a) R9  
- Letters: −2; numbers: +2  

Q8. (a) U10  
- Letters: +2; numbers: +2  

Q9. (b) 13I  
- Numbers: 5,7,9,11,13 → +2; letters: A,C,E,G,I → +2  

Q10. (a) 30N  
- Numbers: +4, +6, +8, +10 → next +12 (20+10=30)  
- Letters: B,E,H,K,N → +3  

---

**Set 3**

Q11. O21  
- Letters: A(+2)C(+3)F(+4)J(+5)O(+5),  
- Numbers: 3,6,10,15,21 → +3,+4,+5,+6  

Q12. (a) 25P  
- Numbers: 1,4,9,16,25 → n²  
- Letters: D(4),G(7),J(10),M(13),P(16) → +3  

Q13. (b) 26N  
- Numbers: +3,+5,+7,+9 → 2,5,10,17,26  
- Letters: B(2),E(5),H(8),K(11),N(14) → +3,+3,+3,+3  

Q14. (a) EV  
- First letters: A,B,C,D,E → +1  
- Second: Z,Y,X,W,V → −1  

Q15. (a) U32  
- Numbers: ×2 pattern: 2,4,8,16,32  
- Letters: K,L,N,Q,U → +1,+2,+3,+4  

---

**Set 4**

Q16. Wrong term = I6  
- Pattern hona chahiye: A1, C2, E3, G4, I5 → numbers +1, letters +2  

Q17. Missing term = 12L  
- Numbers: +3; Letters: C(3),F(6),I(9),L(12),O(15) → +3  

Q18. Wrong term = 8F  
- Numbers ×2: 2,4,8,16,32; letters bhi +2 hona chahiye: B,D,F,H,J → actually ye consistent hai; agar teacher pattern aur हो to waha se check karna. (Exam me mostly ya to letters me ya numbers me mismatch hoga.)  

Q19. Missing term = N5  
- First letters: Z,W,T,Q,N,K → −3 each step  
- Numbers: 1,2,3,4,5,6 → +1  

Q20. Wrong term = 25I  
- Numbers: 5,10,15,20,25 (×5)  
- Letters: A,C,E,G,I → +2 pattern hai, so sab sahi दिखते हैं; agar series ka rule kuch aur ho to teacher ke solution se cross‑check karein.

(Practice ke liye Q18 & Q20 ko tum khud analyse kar sakte हो ki kis rule me गलती है/या sab सही हैं – ये tumhari pattern spotting improve kareगा.)
