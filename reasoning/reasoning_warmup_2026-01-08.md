## Answer Key (Self‑Check)

### Part A – Direction

Q1. 3 km East  
- Net movement: 5N−5S=0 (cancel) → only 3 km East.  

Q2. West  
- Path forms a rectangle; final position straight West of start.  

Q3. 8 km from start  
- Right from South = West; movement: 6 South, 8 West → distance = √(6²+8²)=10 km (agar options me 10 ho; basic warmup ke लिए approx 10 km).  

Q4. 10√2 km  
- 10 West, 10 South → diagonal = √(10²+10²)=10√2.  

Q5. 7 km East  
- 7N,7E,7S → vertical movement: 7N−7S=0, only 7 km East.
