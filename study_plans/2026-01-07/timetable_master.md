## 4. How I&apos;ll Use This File for Your Daily Schedule

- Har nayi date ka `schedule.md` banate waqt main yahin se dekhunga:
  - Subah 7:30 ka **Maths live topic** kya hai  
  - 11:00 ka **English live topic** kya hai  
  - 7:00 pm ka **Reasoning live topic** kya hai  
- Fir uss topic ke hisaab se:
  - Pre‑class warm‑up (`topic_warmups/...`)  
  - Post‑class practice (`study_plans/...` practice files)  
  - Aur agar koi pehle ka backlog hoga to usko bhi include karunga.

Agar timetable me future me change aata hai, tum bas mujhe message karna and main isi `timetable_master.md` ko update kar dunga.
