Part A – One Good Puzzle (20–30 min)
------------------------------------

Puzzle (Linear Seating – 8 persons)
-----------------------------------

Eight friends A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H are sitting in a straight line facing north.  
Use this data to form arrangement:

1. B sits third to the left of F.  
2. Only two persons sit between F and E.  
3. C sits second to the right of E.  
4. A sits at one of the extreme ends.  
5. G is not an immediate neighbour of B.  
6. H sits second to the left of G.  

Task:
- Draw 8 seats (1 to 8) in a line.
- Place A first (on any extreme) and then use clues step by step.
- Try to get a unique arrangement.

After solving:
- Write final order from left to right: __________
- Time taken: __ min
- Check each clue with your final arrangement.

Blood Relation – 5 Questions
----------------------------

Q1. A is the father of B. B is the sister of C. How is C related to A?  
Q2. P is the mother of Q. Q is the brother of R. How is R related to P?  
Q3. X is the son of Y. Y is the daughter of Z. How is Z related to X?  
Q4. M is the husband of N. N is the sister of O. How is O related to M?  
Q5. T is the father of U and V. U is the daughter. What is the relation of V to T?

(Answers: Q1 – son/daughter, Q2 – son/daughter, Q3 – grandparent, Q4 – brother‑in‑law, Q5 – son/daughter)
