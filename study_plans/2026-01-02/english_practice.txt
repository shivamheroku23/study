Part A – RC using Today&apos;s Skimming Technique

Passage (for practice):
-----------------------

Read this short passage with skimming method:

&quot;Over the last few decades, technology has changed the way people communicate. Earlier, letters and landline phones were the primary means of communication. Today, mobile phones and the internet allow people to exchange messages instantly, no matter where they are. This has many advantages, such as saving time and connecting people across the world. However, it has also created problems. Many people spend too much time on social media, which reduces face‑to‑face interaction and affects mental health. Therefore, it is important to use technology wisely and maintain a balance between online and offline life.&quot;

Questions:
1. What is the main idea of the passage?  
   (a) Letters are better than mobile phones  
   (b) Technology has changed communication and needs balanced use  
   (c) Social media should be banned  
   (d) Internet is harmful for everyone  

2. Which of the following was a primary means of communication in earlier times?  
   (a) Mobile phones  
   (b) Social media  
   (c) Letters  
   (d) Video calls  

3. According to the passage, what is one problem of too much social media use?  
   (a) Increase in travelling cost  
   (b) Less face‑to‑face interaction  
   (c) People stop using the internet  
   (d) More letter writing  

4. The author&apos;s tone in the passage is:  
   (a) Completely negative towards technology  
   (b) Completely positive towards technology  
   (c) Balanced – both advantages and disadvantages  
   (d) Confused
