Simplification &amp; Approximation – Pre‑Class Warm‑up
===================================================

Based on: old 2025 PDFs – &quot;Simplification &amp; Approximation Part 1–8&quot;

Goal (20–25 min before LIVE class)
----------------------------------
- Refresh basic operations so that class के questions smooth लगें।
- Mind को +, −, ×, ÷, %, fractions, decimals की आदत में लाना।

Block–3: Quick Approximations (5–7 min)
---------------------------------------

Round smartly (nearest integer):

21)  199.6 ÷ 9.8 ≈ ______  
22)  29.9% of 401 ≈ ______  
23)  18.1 × 4.9 ≈ ______  
24)  √(799) ≈ ______  
25)  49.8 ÷ 5.2 ≈ ______  

Hint:
- 199.6 ≈ 200, 9.8 ≈ 10 → 200 ÷ 10 ≈ 20  
- 29.9% ≈ 30%, 18.1 ≈ 18, 4.9 ≈ 5, 799 ≈ 800 etc.

How to Use
----------

- LIVE se पहले:
